--- ENGLISH ---
* DUMA does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data. It has no accounts, no
  analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
* The game stores saves, statistics, achievements and settings (language, volume, theme)
  locally on your device (browser/WebView localStorage). This data never leaves the device
  and is deleted when you clear the app's data or use the reset buttons in the game.
* Fonts are bundled with the game; it makes no requests to font or CDN servers.
* The only outbound link is an optional link to the developer's Telegram channel in the
  Credits screen. It opens in your browser only if you tap it; Telegram's own privacy policy
  then applies.
* Children: the game is a political satire intended for older teens and adults and does not
  knowingly collect any information from anyone.
* If distributed through a store or portal (itch.io, VK Play, RuStore, etc.), that platform may
  collect its own data under its own policy.
